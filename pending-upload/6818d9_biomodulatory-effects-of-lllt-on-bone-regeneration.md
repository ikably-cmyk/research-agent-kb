**Resultado principal:** Acelerar la regeneración ósea en etapas tempranas de cicatrización post-cirugía y mejorar la calidad de la osteointegración de implantes dentales mediante irradiación con láser de diodo 830 nm de baja intensidad.

- **Area of newly formed mineralized bone at 7 days (rat femur defect)**: Irradiated group: mean area 2,852,629.12 µm²; Non-irradiated: 1,561,740.66 µm² _(statistically significant; clinical: Significant increase in early bone formation at 7 days post-surgery with LLLT 830 nm)_
- **Area of newly formed mineralized bone at 28 days (rat femur defect)**: Irradiated group: mean area 861,794.15 µm²; Non-irradiated: 655,798.96 µm² _(not statistically significant; clinical: No significant difference at 28 days; LLLT effect appears limited to early healing stages)_
- **Bone quality at implant/bone interface (SEM, dogs, 45 and 60 days)**: Irradiated specimens showed lamellar re-arrangement, higher neovascularization, and more mature bone at the implant interface compared to non-irradiated controls _(clinical: Qualitative SEM improvement in osseointegration quality with LLLT 830 nm)_

**Nivel de evidencia:** Bajo — estudios en modelos animales (rata y perro) con grupos pequeños; sin RCT en humanos. Resultados estadísticamente significativos solo en etapa temprana (7 días) en el modelo de rata.
