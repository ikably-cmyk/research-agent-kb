**Resultado principal:** Eradicar biofilms endodónticos de E. faecalis y C. albicans (mono y mixtos) mediante la activación de Zn(II)chlorin e6 methyl ester con luz LED roja (627 nm), generando especies reactivas de oxígeno que dañan irreversiblemente las membranas celulares microbianas. Ofrece una alternativa de menor toxicidad tisular que el NaOCl con eficacia comparable a CHX y EDTA.

- **Biofilm biomass removal — E. faecalis monospecies**: Zn(II)e6Me removed ~60% biomass at 60 and 90 s; more effective than TBO and RB (P=0.0079); similar to TMPyP _(statistically significant; clinical: Comparable to CHX and EDTA; inferior to NaOCl (70–90%))_
- **Biofilm biomass removal — C. albicans monospecies**: Zn(II)e6Me and TMPyP had similar efficacy at 90 s; Zn(II)e6Me more effective than TBO (P=0.0317) and RB (P=0.0079) at 90 s _(statistically significant; clinical: Similar to CHX and EDTA; inferior to NaOCl)_
- **Biofilm biomass removal — mixed E. faecalis + C. albicans biofilm**: Zn(II)e6Me removed 58.98% biomass (P<0.001); most efficient PS tested; mixed biofilm less susceptible than monospecies _(statistically significant; clinical: Similar to CHX and EDTA; inferior to NaOCl; mixed biofilms represent a more challenging clinical scenario)_
- **Dark toxicity of photosensitizers (15 min incubation, no light)**: Biofilm reduction ranged from 5.7% (Zn(II)e6Me, mixed) to 16.6% (TBO, C. albicans); Zn(II)e6Me showed lowest dark activity _(clinical: Low dark toxicity is desirable to ensure antimicrobial effect is light-dependent)_
- **Ultrastructural changes in E. faecalis after aPDT with Zn(II)e6Me**: Cell wall 'ghosts' observed — intact cell walls devoid of intracellular content; sporadic cell wall destruction _(clinical: Indicates cytoplasmic membrane damage leading to leakage of cellular contents)_
- **Ultrastructural changes in C. albicans after aPDT with Zn(II)e6Me**: Irregular cell wall thickness, cytoplasmic membrane disruption with invaginations, ER whorls, large vacuoles with electrodense materials _(clinical: Indicates intracellular damage consistent with autophagy and organelle disruption)_

**Nivel de evidencia:** Bajo — estudio in vitro con modelo de biofilm en placa de 96 pocillos; sin modelo de diente humano ni ensayo clínico. Zn(II)e6Me no está disponible comercialmente.
