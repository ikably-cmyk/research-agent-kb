**Resultado principal:** Prevenir y tratar la mucositis oral inducida por quimiorradioterapia en pacientes con cáncer de cabeza y cuello, mediante la aplicación de luz roja o infrarroja cercana que modula la respuesta inflamatoria, promueve la cicatrización tisular y reduce el dolor.

- **Oral mucositis prevention and treatment in HNC and HSCT patients**: Systematic reviews support efficacy of PBM for OM management _(statistically significant; clinical: PBM reduces severity and incidence of oral mucositis in patients undergoing chemoradiotherapy for HNC)_
- **Effect of PBM on tumor cell proliferation in vitro**: Conflicting results across different tumor cell lines and PBM parameters; some studies show proliferation, others show inhibition or no effect _(clinical: Clinical significance unclear; in vitro findings may not translate to in vivo tumor behavior)_
- **Locoregional disease control and survival in HNC patients receiving PBM for OM prevention during CRT**: At median follow-up of 18 months, patients treated with PBM had better locoregional disease control and improved progression-free or overall survival (one RCT, ref 99) _(statistically significant; clinical: Suggests PBM does not negatively impact and may improve oncologic outcomes)_
- **Carcinogenic effects of PBM on normal cells**: No signs of malignant transformation in non-malignant epithelial cells and fibroblasts following PBM exposure _(clinical: PBM appears unlikely to have carcinogenic effects on normal cells)_

**Nivel de evidencia:** Moderado — múltiples revisiones sistemáticas y meta-análisis apoyan la eficacia de PBM para mucositis oral en HNC y HSCT; sin embargo, la heterogeneidad de parámetros y la falta de estandarización limitan las conclusiones definitivas.
