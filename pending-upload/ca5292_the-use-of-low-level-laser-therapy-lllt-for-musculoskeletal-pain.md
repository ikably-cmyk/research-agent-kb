**Resultado principal:** Reducir el dolor agudo y crónico musculoesquelético, disminuir la inflamación y el edema, e inducir analgesia mediante la aplicación de luz roja o infrarroja cercana de baja intensidad sobre el sitio de lesión, nodos linfáticos, nervios periféricos y puntos gatillo.

- **Pain reduction in musculoskeletal conditions**: Majority of >4000 laboratory and clinical studies demonstrate positive effect on acute and chronic musculoskeletal pain _(clinical: Broad evidence base across sprains, tendinitis, osteoarthritis, neck/back pain, neuropathic pain, and dental pain conditions)_
- **Neural blockade onset**: Approximately 30% neural blockade within 10–20 minutes of application, reversed within ~24 hours _(clinical: Fast-acting pain relief mechanism via peripheral nerve inhibition)_
- **Anti-inflammatory effect**: Reduction of inflammatory cells, pro-inflammatory cytokines, and edema demonstrated in animal models and clinical trials _(clinical: Medium-term effect (hours to days); relevant for acute and chronic inflammatory conditions)_

**Nivel de evidencia:** Bajo — revisión narrativa sin datos primarios propios; se basa en >400 RCTs y >4000 estudios de laboratorio citados, pero no se realiza meta-análisis ni síntesis sistemática en este paper.
