**Resultado principal:** Irradiación con láser diodo de alta potencia (808 nm) dentro de la bolsa periodontal como coadyuvante al raspado y alisado radicular, con el objetivo de reducir la carga bacteriana subgingival y mejorar los parámetros clínicos periodontales.

- **Clinical Probing Depth (CPD) reduction at 6 weeks**: Control group: 2.4 mm reduction (P<0.001); Experimental group: 2.1 mm reduction (P<0.001); significant difference between groups favoring control (P=0.014) _(statistically significant; clinical: Conventional SRP alone produced greater CPD reduction than SRP + diode laser)_
- **Clinical Attachment Level (CAL) at 6 weeks**: Control group: 1.9 mm gain (P<0.001); Experimental group: 1.2 mm gain (P<0.001); significant difference between groups favoring control (P=0.039) _(statistically significant; clinical: Conventional SRP alone produced greater CAL gain than SRP + diode laser)_
- **Total bacterial CFU at 6 weeks**: Both groups showed significant reduction (P<0.001 and P=0.005); no significant difference between groups (P=0.55) _(not statistically significant; clinical: Diode laser did not provide additional microbiological benefit)_
- **Dark-pigmented bacteria CFU at 6 weeks**: Both groups reduced significantly; no significant difference between groups (P=0.30) _(not statistically significant; clinical: No additional bactericidal effect from diode laser on pigmented bacteria)_
- **A. actinomycetemcomitans CFU at 6 weeks**: Reduction in both groups; no significant difference between groups (P=0.10) _(not statistically significant; clinical: No additional benefit from diode laser on A. actinomycetemcomitans)_
- **Plaque Index (PI) and Bleeding on Probing (BP)**: Significant reduction in both groups (P<0.001); no significant difference between groups _(not statistically significant; clinical: No additional benefit from diode laser on PI or BP)_

**Nivel de evidencia:** Moderado — RCT split-mouth doble ciego con N=27; resultado negativo (sin beneficio adicional del láser diodo sobre RAR convencional con los parámetros utilizados).
