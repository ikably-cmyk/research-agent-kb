**Resultado principal:** Ablación fotodinámica no mutilante de carcinomas escamosos superficiales (estadio I) y lesiones premalignas asociadas (leucoplasia, eritroplasia) en pacientes con campo de cancerización oral, preservando la función y permitiendo repetición del tratamiento sin pérdida de tejido normal.

- **Complete response rate at 6–8 weeks**: 10/11 patients (91%) showed complete response (no evidence of abnormal mucosa clinically or histologically) _(clinical: High clinical relevance for management of multifocal oral field cancerization without mutilating surgery)_
- **Recurrence of invasive carcinoma in treated areas**: No patient developed recurrent invasive carcinoma in treated areas during follow-up (3–19 months) _(clinical: Promising short-term local control, though longer follow-up needed)_
- **Metachronous primary carcinoma development**: 2/11 patients developed metachronous primaries at sites away from treated areas _(clinical: Reflects field change disease biology, not PDT failure)_
- **Healing time of treated areas**: 3–5 weeks depending on area size _(clinical: Acceptable healing time for outpatient procedure)_

**Nivel de evidencia:** Bajo — serie de casos prospectiva con n=11, sin grupo control, seguimiento máximo 19 meses (media 11 meses). Estudio de 1993 con fotosensibilizador de primera generación.
