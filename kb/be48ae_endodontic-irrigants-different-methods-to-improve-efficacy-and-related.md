## Outcomes reportados

_Los outcomes siguientes provienen del paper como conjunto. Consultar el documento original para asociar cada outcome al protocolo o intervención específica._

- **Reaction rate of irrigant activation methods**: Heating, PUI, UNI, LAI, and PIPS all increase reaction rate; mechanical activation, sonic, and EndoVac do not _(clinical: Increased reaction rate improves antimicrobial efficacy and organic tissue dissolution)_
- **Shear stress on canal walls**: All activation methods except heating increase shear stress _(clinical: Higher shear stress improves smear layer removal)_
- **Vapor lock effect**: Mechanical activation, ultrasonic (PUI/UNI), sonic, LAI, PIPS, and EndoVac all reduce vapor lock; heating does not _(clinical: Vapor lock reduction allows irrigant to reach apical third)_
- **Apical extrusion risk**: UNI, LAI, and PIPS increase apical extrusion risk; EndoVac reduces it; PUI and sonic do not influence from 2 mm apex _(clinical: Apical extrusion of NaOCl can cause severe periapical tissue damage)_
